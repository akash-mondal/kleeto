# Kleeto landing — revision 2 (product-forward)

Direction change from the user, in their words: the copy should "focus like a product and not
as a project for Hedera"; lead with "bringing a full desktop to the agent and browser as well";
stay "away from just bare machines"; metering and receipts are "included and not in the copy";
use the logos; bring the product-page hero art and animations over; and put a real screen
recording of an agent working under the hero.

## What changes
1. **Hero** leads with the desktop and the browser, not with payment. The meter HUD stays —
   it is the product working, not a pitch.
2. **A real recording sits under the hero**: 43 s, four windows, unedited except for speed.
   `public/video/workspace.{mp4,webm}` + poster. Shot on a live desktop lane; the terminal in
   it prints that lease's own elapsed seconds and this lane's own published rate.
3. **Lane order is desktop → browser → machine.** The machine card is where capability gets
   advertised (installs, renders, servers, preview URLs), not where the pitch starts.
4. **Logos are used**: a "runs what you already run" strip of real brand marks, plus the app
   dock in the desktop showcase.
5. **Two new showcase sections** carry the product-page art and loops: Desktop (screenshots,
   app dock, takeover) and Browser (humanizer cursor, headless/headful).
6. **Metering, receipts and the ledger move down** and lose the sales voice. One factual
   mention each. No "x402", "Hedera", "HCS" above the fold; they stay in the FAQ, the receipt
   section and the docs, where a developer expects them.
