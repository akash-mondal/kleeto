## Fusion direction for Kleeto
Take **Solari's dark surface and amber accent** (it reads as infrastructure, and the benchmark
bars are the closest existing device to our meter) and **Runloop's structural rhythm** — pill
eyebrows, boxed highlight word in headings, generous radii, tight small type — which is far more
legible than Solari's very long dark scroll.
Kleeto's own additions: the live meter HUD, per-lane price cards in USD, and a receipt/verify
block that neither source has.
