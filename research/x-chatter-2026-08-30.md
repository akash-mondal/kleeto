## 3. Implications for hedera-x402sandbox
- Catalog by *type*, not brand. The market has stratified into: (a) ephemeral microVM tool-exec, (b) persistent computer-per-agent, (c) desktop/browser, (d) cheap isolate tier, (e) RL fleet fan-out, (f) SaaS-replica API sandboxes.
- Since every harness now treats the sandbox as a plugin (Hermes, DeepSeek Harness, Vercel HarnessAgent, Mastra, TanStack, Sandbox SDK), an x402 marketplace should expose itself as *one of those plugin backends* — the agent's harness picks "x402 marketplace" as its sandbox provider and pays per second.
- Lead with isolation tier + egress policy + credential brokering in the listing schema; that's what buyers ask about this month.
- The self-hoster segment (OpenClaw/Hermes/Grok Bot users) is loud and account-averse — x402 "no signup" is the wedge.
